Overlay configuration
Match Bar (Stats, Player Names, Gametime)
   
  • The Match Bar is used to show information about you and your opponent: Name, Alias (BNet-Name), Statistics, Country. Additionally you can show the current game duration.
  • If you dont set a name, your Twitch username is show in the Match Bar.

Map Bar
   
  • Shows the sessions match score and the map name (alternating) above the minimap.
Control Groups
   
  • Control groups (first unit of the group + group size) in the bottom center of the screen.

Hero Abilities
 
  • Show the skilled abilities of an hero right beside the hero icon in the top left.
Hero Items
 
  • Show the hero inventory next to the hero's avatar.
Hero Experience
 
  • Show a progress bar regarding reaching the next level. Placed on the left of the hero icon.
Hero Level
 
  • Show the hero level above the avatar of the hero. Includes one digit indicating the level progress (e.g. "1.7" for level 1 and 70% to level 2 )
 Researches
 
  • Show researched upgrades on the top of the screen (like armor- and attack upgrades)
  • Options
    Options are not available until you enabled the Match Bar
